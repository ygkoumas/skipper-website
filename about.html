My Sea Journey

Born in Greece, Yannis developed a deep connection to the sea. From an early age, he practiced windsurfing, which sparked a lifelong passion for wind and sea. His experience on sailing yachts spans a wide range of sailing areas, both as a skipper and instructor.

As a skipper, he creates memorable and authentic experiences at sea, tailored to each crew and journey.

As a sailing instructor, he is enthusiastic about helping people become confident sailors and discover the freedom that comes from being on the water.

He is committed to making time on the water enjoyable while always respecting wildlife and others enjoying the sea.
